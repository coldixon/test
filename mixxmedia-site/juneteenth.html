The audience

Nobody lands here by accident.

Every visitor arrived searching for a Juneteenth event. They have already decided to celebrate — they are choosing how, where, and with whom.

That is a rare thing to be able to say about an audience. There is no general-interest waste to pay for, and no guessing at intent. Traffic concentrates in the cities where the day is biggest: Houston, Atlanta, Washington DC, Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, Detroit, Philadelphia, Dallas, and Oakland.

  • National and local in one buy. A brand can reach 50 cities; a single festival can reach exactly its own.
  • Independent and credible. Not a corporate aggregator — a community resource that visitors and event producers actually trust.
  • Multi-channel. Web, newsletter, and social are bundled into most packages.
  • Reporting built in. Every campaign returns impressions, click-through, and conversion data where available.
  • Editorial guardrails. We do not run sketchy ads. Your brand sits beside vetted festivals, museums, and civic events.
  • Peak intent, narrow window. Traffic climbs through May and spikes into June 19 — campaigns are timed, not always-on.
  • Mobile-first. Most visitors are planning on a phone, often on the day itself.
  • Capped inventory. Six featured slots per city, so a campaign is never buried under twenty others.
