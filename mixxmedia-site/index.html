Inklings®

Invented by a fourth grader and a second grader.

In spring 2020, Joshua and Lauren Dixon ran out of card games they liked. So they made one — drawing the characters themselves, arguing over what each card should be worth, and testing the rules on their family.

Inklings is a game of memory, math, and a little bit of mischief. Fifty-four cards, two to six players, twenty minutes. Avoid the ink, protect your hand, and finish with the highest score. It was featured on Good Morning America, and the math program that grew out of it now maps to Common Core standards from kindergarten through third grade.

JuneteenthEvents.us

Where the country finds its Juneteenth.

Every June, millions of Americans look for a way to mark the day in their own city. JuneteenthEvents.us is where they land.

Parades, festivals, concerts, and community programming across 50 cities, in one place. It is independent — not a corporate aggregator — and it is used by both the people celebrating and the organizers trying to reach them.

  • 50 cities covered nationwide, led by Houston, Atlanta, DC, Chicago, and New York
  • An audience that has already decided to celebrate — they are choosing how, where, and with whom
  • Sponsorship and advertising from single-event features to season-long brand partnerships
