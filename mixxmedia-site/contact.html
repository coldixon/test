About

MixxMedia, LLC

A family-founded media and game studio based in Upper Marlboro, Maryland.

We started with a card game that two kids invented during the spring of 2020 — and the whole company grew outward from the same instinct: make the thing you wish existed, then do the unglamorous work of getting it into people’s hands.

Today that means original games for young learners, the curriculum that lets teachers use them, digital titles and animated shorts in development, a national platform for Juneteenth celebrations, and consulting work for teams trying to do something similar.
