Why we do this

We got tired of the PDF.

Most youth tournaments still run on a spreadsheet exported to PDF, emailed on a Thursday, and revised twice before Saturday.

We know because we have been the family refreshing an inbox in a parking lot. A tournament is a live thing — games move, sheets change, a division gets re-seeded — and the people who need that information most are standing in a building with bad signal and a kid to get to warm-ups.

So we build the other version: one link, always current, fast on a phone, and legible to a grandparent who has never used it before.

Capabilities

What goes into an event site.

Mix and match — a single weekend shootout needs less than a season-long league.

Schedules & brackets

Every game with division, rink or field, time, and matchup. Round robin through to championship brackets, updated the moment a result lands.

Live scores & standings

Scores entered once and reflected everywhere — standings, tiebreakers, and the bracket recalculated automatically.

Team & division pages

A shareable page per team: their games, their results, their next puck drop. The link a coach can send to twenty families and never explain.

Per-game cards generated straight from the schedule data — matchup graphics for social, scoreboard overlays, and printable sheets for the venue.

How we think about it

Three constraints that decide every design call.

One

Phone first, signal second

Rinks and fields have terrible connectivity. Pages stay small, load fast, and keep working when the bars drop to one.

Two

One source of truth

A change is made once, by the tournament director, and propagates to every schedule, bracket, team page, and graphic. No second spreadsheet.

Three

Readable at a glance

Big type, clear division labels, obvious rink numbers. Someone should be able to answer "where and when" in under three seconds.

Who it’s for

Events, leagues, and the people who run them.

  • Tournament directors running single-weekend showcases and shootouts
  • Leagues and associations managing a full season across divisions
  • Rinks, clubs, and facilities hosting multiple events a year
  • Video and photography partners who need their work to land somewhere useful
  • Sponsors looking for placement in front of an audience that shows up all weekend
