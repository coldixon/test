Proof

What that looked like on our own products.

A game into a thousand homes

Inklings went from a kitchen-table prototype to national retail and a Good Morning America feature, with 85% of buyers rating it four or five stars.

A game into a curriculum

The Inklings Math Program maps the game to Common Core standards across kindergarten through third grade, built with advisors from K–12 teaching, university computer science, and AWS Global Educate.

A directory into a media property

JuneteenthEvents.us grew to national coverage across 50 cities with a rate card spanning $50 event features to five-figure annual brand sponsorships.
